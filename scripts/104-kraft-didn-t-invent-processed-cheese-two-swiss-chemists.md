## 01 Cold Open

Ask where processed cheese came from and a lot of people will say Kraft, and James L. Kraft, a Chicago cheese salesman, really did patent a process for it in the United States in 1916, but two Swiss chemists, Walter Gerber and Fritz Stettler, had worked out a way to make it around 1911, though some accounts say 1912 or 1913, and they did it a different way. Now, I'm not saying Kraft borrowed anything from anybody, as far as the literature says he was working independently, and we'll get to that.

## 02 Kraft's Patent

Kraft filed his patent on March 25th, 1916, and the title was Process of sterilizing cheese and an improved product produced by such process. What it describes is heat plus continuous stirring of Cheddar cheese, and the goal was to be able to keep the cheese indefinitely. That was the first US patent for the process, so Kraft really does have that, and nothing I say about the Swiss takes it away from him.

## 03 Gerber and Stettler

The Swiss version goes like this. In Thun, in Switzerland, two chemists, Walter Gerber and Fritz Stettler, melted shredded Emmentaler cheese with sodium citrate, and the result was a smooth cheese that kept better and held together when heated. The date most sources give is 1911, and that's what a peer-reviewed review in the International Dairy Journal says, and a paper from Michigan State University Extension says the same, but some accounts say 1912 or 1913, so I'm going to say around 1911. The aim, as it's described, was cheese that lasted longer when it was shipped to warm places.

## 04 Two Different Recipes

Now here's the part that matters. The Swiss recipe used sodium citrate to keep the melted cheese smooth, but Kraft's 1916 patent used only heat and constant stirring of Cheddar, with no sodium citrate in it. So as far as the literature says, Kraft was working independently, he got to processed cheese on his own and without the Swiss ingredient, right? I'm not going to say he copied anybody, because nothing I found says that.

## 05 Close

So the plain version is, two Swiss chemists, Gerber and Stettler, melted Emmentaler with sodium citrate around 1911, and Kraft, a Chicago cheese salesman, filed the first US patent for a processed cheese process in March of 1916, using heat and stirring and Cheddar. Somebody did it first, and Kraft arrived at it by himself, as far as the literature says.
