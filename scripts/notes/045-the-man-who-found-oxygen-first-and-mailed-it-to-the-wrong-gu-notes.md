Plain explanation added (no new claims): phlogiston glossed as the substance chemists then thought burning gave off; apothecary glossed as a pharmacist; "fire air" explained as the part of air that keeps a fire going; "about two months after Priestley" is August 1 to September 30; publishing first is usually how a name gets attached.

Hedges kept: "around 1771 to 1772" (sources differ), "as far as anyone knows", "there's no record he replied or credited it", "we don't know whether he read the letter", "I'm not going to say he did" (no theft claim), "usually shared three ways", "first to make it, last to get credit".
