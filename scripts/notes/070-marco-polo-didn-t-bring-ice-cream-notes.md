## Review
- Fact fixes: (1) Cold open said "the first people to write that trick down were doctors and scholars". The entry says the Arab physicians recorded only that saltpetre chills water, not the salt-and-ice freezing trick, and nothing establishes who was first to write the trick down. Now "the people who wrote down the science behind that trick". (2) Zimara (1530) comes only from Jurafsky's blog, the same single source as the Arab physicians, but wasn't attributed; now "again going by Jurafsky". (3) The close said "della Porta was freezing wine"; the entry says he was describing how to, so it now says "describing how to freeze wine". (4) "filled in by the historians" pinned the inference on unnamed historians; now "filled in rather than written down". Arithmetic checks: 1530 - 1029 = 501 ("about five hundred years"); 1558 to the 1690s is "more than a century". Title is supported.
