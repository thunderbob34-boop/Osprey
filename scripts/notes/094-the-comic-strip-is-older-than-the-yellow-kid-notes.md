## Review
- Fact fixes: (1) "an unauthorized English version of the Vieux Bois story" named which Töpffer story Oldbuck adapted; the entry doesn't say, so now "one of Töpffer's stories". (2) "the comic strip form ... starts in Switzerland" overclaimed an absolute origin; now "our story starts in Switzerland". (3) "the idea of telling a story in a sequence of drawings" credited Töpffer with inventing sequential art itself, and "Britannica ... gives him his place in it for exactly that" overstated the source; both reworded. (4) Close restored the form-vs-newspaper hedge ("for the comic strip form, as opposed to the newspaper strip") and swapped the crafted "without him ever being asked" for the plain 1842 fact. (5) Hedged the speculation on why the credit stuck ("I think").
- Cadence fixes: 2. Cold open "wasn't the first comic Americans ever read. Fifty-three years before..." joined into one breath; back-to-back "Now, to be fair" in sections 04 and 05 varied to "And to be fair".
