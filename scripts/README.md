# Scripts: "Somebody Did It First"

All 100 recording scripts. Episode 1 is `../launch-episode-script.md`; this folder has 2–100. Each script is one file in your recording format: the title, then READING NOTES, then numbered sections containing only spoken words. Each one has a matching file in `notes/` that maps every claim to its sources, lists the hedges kept and what was left out on purpose, and logs the review.

## How these were made

- **Sources:** every script was written only from that episode's verified research entry (`episode-research.md` or `100-episode-lineup.md`). No facts were added from memory, and anything the research marked unverified or "keep off air" was left out.
- **Model:** the launch script set the format, voice and hedging discipline.
- **Review:** a fresh reviewer then checked each batch of ten against its research. It fixed every date, number and name that didn't trace to the research, restored hedges that had been dropped, and checked "X years before" arithmetic. It also ran a cadence pass against your speech samples (joining choppy lines, removing crafted endings).
- **Titles:** about 30 titles were changed where the old one claimed more than the research supports. File names now match the corrected titles.

## Read this before recording

- **These run short.** Total 82,765 spoken words across 99 scripts, an average of about 836 per script, which is roughly 5 minutes at your pace (range ~4–6.5 minutes), not the 8–10 you targeted. The research entries hold about that much verified material, and the writers were told never to pad. To reach 8–10 minutes, each episode needs a second round of research first. The "Open check" column flags where the gaps are.
- **One last pass per script:** before you record any of them, run it through the same final-read and fact-check pass the launch script got. The review pass here is one round, not the launch's 13.
- **Pronunciations:** names marked as a guess in a script's reading notes (e.g. Magie, Nowak, Tawell, Chevedden) need checking.
