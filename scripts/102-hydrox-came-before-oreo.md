# Hydrox Came Before Oreo

- Tone is light and matter-of-fact. This one is told with dates only, so let the dates carry it and do not add any attitude about Oreo.
- Say Hydrox as HY-drox, Nabisco as NAB-iss-co, KCUR as K-C-U-R, Loose-Wiles as LOOSE-WYLZ, Hoboken as HO-bo-ken.

## 01 Cold Open

Oreo is widely treated as the original chocolate cream sandwich cookie, but as far as these sources go it wasn't the first cookie of its kind. A Kansas City company had one on shelves in 1908, and the Oreo came four years later, in 1912, and that first cookie was called Hydrox.

## 02 The Famous Version

The Oreo is Nabisco's cookie, and according to Knowledge at Wharton, the business school publication from the University of Pennsylvania, the first Oreo sale was on March 6th, 1912, and it was to a grocer in Hoboken, New Jersey. So if you treat the Oreo as the original, which a lot of people do, then 1912 is where the story starts, and that's the version most of us have heard.

## 03 Hydrox

Hydrox was introduced in 1908 by Sunshine Biscuits of Kansas City, and that same Wharton article says Hydrox was actually introduced first, in 1908. NPR has it the same way, Hydrox in 1908 and Oreo in 1912, and KCUR, the Kansas City public radio station, also puts the debut in 1908 and says it came from a Kansas City company. So as far as these sources go, Hydrox was the first chocolate sandwich cookie of its kind in the US, though I can't give you the month it came out, because the sources I found give the year and not the month.

## 04 The Company Behind It

Now, about the name Sunshine, because it's a little more layered than that. The Kansas City Public Library's history collection says the Loose-Wiles Biscuit Company was formed in Kansas City in 1902 by the Loose brothers and a partner named John Wiles, and that Sunshine was the trade name on its principal product, crackers, and then the company took the Sunshine name in 1947. So when I say Sunshine Biscuits made Hydrox, that's the name the sources use, and the company at the time of the 1908 launch was Loose-Wiles.

## 05 What We Can Say

Now, here's the part where I have to be careful. What the sources give us is the dates, Hydrox in 1908 and the Oreo in 1912, so the fair way to say it is that the Oreo came four years later, and that's all. The dates don't tell us whether the Oreo was a response to Hydrox, right? And I'd rather say less than say something I can't back up.

## 06 What Happened to Hydrox

Hydrox did not stay on the shelves. It went out of production, and sources differ on when, some say 1999 and some say 2003, so I'll say 1999 or 2003 and leave it there. Then in 2015, according to NPR, a company called Leaf Brands brought Hydrox back, which was more than a century after it first came out.

## 07 Close

So here's the plain version. Hydrox, from Sunshine Biscuits of Kansas City, came out in 1908, and the Oreo came four years later, in 1912. As far as these sources go, Hydrox was the first chocolate sandwich cookie of its kind in the US, and somebody did it first, and it was a Kansas City company.
