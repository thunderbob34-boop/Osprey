# Tonight: recording Episode 1 (Trevithick)

**You need:** the prep sheet, a mic (your usual one or your phone), headphones, water, and about 45 minutes.

**You don't need yet:**
- music;
- a Spotify account;
- show artwork.

Those come before publishing, not before recording. I add the music in the edit.

## 1. Set up (10 minutes)
- **Room.** Use the smallest, softest room you have: a closet full of clothes, or a bedroom with the bed and curtains. Avoid a kitchen or an empty room with bare walls (echo).
- **Kill the noise.** Turn off fans, the furnace or AC if you can, and the TV. Put your phone on Do Not Disturb. Keep water at room temperature, with no ice clinking.
- **If you're using your usual mic into the computer:**
  - Record WAV at 48 kHz.
  - Put your mouth 4–6 inches away, slightly off to the side so "p" sounds don't pop.
  - Keep the loudest parts around the middle to upper meter, never in the red.
- **If you're using your phone:**
  - On an iPhone, check Settings > Voice Memos > Audio Quality and set it to Lossless.
  - Prop the phone on books or a stand, not in your hand, 6–8 inches from your mouth, slightly off to the side.

## 2. Test (2 minutes)
1. Record 15 seconds of silence (room tone). It helps me clean the noise out later.
2. Read the cold open once.
3. Play it back on headphones. Listen for hiss, echo and popping. Fix anything you hear now; it's much harder to fix later.

## 3. Record, in this order
Keep the recording rolling the whole time. Say the section number out loud before each one ("Section four") so I can find it.

| Order | Section | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **2: The story everyone knows** | Scripted, so it warms you up. |
| 2 | **3: "But somebody did it first."** | Three takes, each with different energy. Leave a beat of silence before it. |
| 3 | **4: Who really did it** | In your words, from the bullets. Looking down at the sheet is fine. |
| 4 | **5: Why history forgot him** | Pick 2–3 questions and talk. This is where the fun lives. |
| 5 | **5b: Legend or Fact?** | Make your real guess out loud before each reveal. |
| 6 | **6: What we know vs. the legend** | Say each hedge once. |
| 7 | **Credit rating, then 7: the verdict** | Rate it Stolen, Shared or Earned, give one line of why, then read the verdict. |
| 8 | **8: Next time** | Use the Doctors' Day tease. |
| 9 | **1: Intro** | Three takes. |
| 10 | **0: Cold open** | Last, now that you know how it felt. Two or three takes. |

**When you mess up:** don't stop. Pause two seconds, then start that sentence again. I cut the bad take.

**Energy:**
- Stand up if you can.
- Talk to one person; picture your dad across the table.
- Smile on the story parts; people can hear it.

## 4. Send it
1. Name the file `ep01-raw.wav` (or `.m4a` from the phone). If you made several files, number them: `ep01-raw-01`, `ep01-raw-02`…
2. Attach it in this chat. If it's too big to attach, put it in Google Drive and paste the share link here.

## 5. What I do once it's in
1. **Transcribe and fact-check** against the prep sheet. I flag anything to cut or fix, with the timestamp.
2. **Rough edit:** cut flubs and long pauses, put the sections in episode order, level the audio, and add placeholder music.
3. **Write up:** chapters, show notes with sources, and 2–3 picks for Shorts, including the Legend or Fact? round.
4. **Send you the rough cut** to listen to.
